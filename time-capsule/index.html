About me

Hi! I'm Faye. I made my first video at 8: Monster High dolls, stop motion, done badly. Then came Minecraft, then vlogs, then music. I never really stopped.

Now I'm a graphic designer in Berlin, and all of those habits turned into the job. I design brand systems, run social media, and shoot, animate and cut video. If you're hiring for any of that, this page is the long version of my CV.

Who I'd like to meet: a team that makes things for an audience and cares how they look. Bonus points if you remember the Monster High webisodes.

minecraft, but with logos

Date: 2014 and on · Mood: determined · Category: gaming, animation

I ran a Minecraft channel where every series had its own look: Hunger Games, Skyblock, Capture the Flag. I rendered my own character in Cinema 4D for the thumbnails, and I hit 300 subscribers in October 2014.

Then I started making parodies. I wrote the lyrics, recorded them and animated the whole thing myself. Herobrine's Revenge, my first animation, reached 1,092 views, and my Break Free parody reached 2,127.

so here I am (then and now)

Date: 2026 · Mood: employable · Category: work

I never stopped making things for an audience. I just got paid for it and picked better fonts. It's the same instinct as that Minecraft thumbnail: one clear image, one big line, readable on a phone. These days it's brand systems, social posts, Reels, thumbnails and video.
